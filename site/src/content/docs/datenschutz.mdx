## In short

This website sets no cookies, uses no tracking and no analytics, and loads nothing from other
servers. Fonts, scripts and the search all come from this website itself. Personal data only
arises from hosting: GitHub, which runs GitHub Pages, logs the IP addresses of visitors.

## Hosting on GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street,
San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, GitHub processes the data that is technically
needed for it, including your IP address, the date and time, the address requested, the referrer
and your browser's identifier. According to GitHub, it logs the IP addresses of visitors to keep
the service secure and to meet legal obligations.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. The legitimate interest is to provide this website
securely and reliably. GitHub decides how long it keeps the logs; we have no access to them.

This transfers data to the USA. According to GitHub, it takes part in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy
Framework, for which the EU Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR). See
[GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for details.

## Storage in your browser

If you choose a light or dark colour scheme, the website remembers it in your browser's local
storage (`localStorage`). It remembers which sidebar sections are open until you close the tab
(`sessionStorage`). This stays on your device and is only stored because you use the website that
way (§ 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete it in your browser at any time.

## Links to other websites

The downloads, the source code and the drivers are on GitHub. If you follow such a link, that
provider's privacy policy applies.

## Contact by email

If you email us, we process your address and your message to answer you (Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f)
GDPR) and delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

## Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17),
restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based
on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can also complain to a data protection supervisory
authority (Art. 77 GDPR).
